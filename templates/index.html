About BMM Translations

BMM Translations is a real-time, browser-based translation platform designed to empower organizations of all sizes to reach multilingual audiences—without the need for expensive hardware, complex installations, or high licensing fees.

Whether you're hosting events, services, workshops, or conferences, BMM Translations makes it easy to connect speakers and listeners across languages through live audio and text translations.

What We Offer

👥

Large Audience Support

Capable of supporting hundreds of concurrent users across multiple devices without sacrificing performance.

🌐

No App Required

Both speakers and audience members join directly through their browsers. No downloads, no setup—just a link or QR code.

📱

Quick Listener Access

Audience members can join sessions effortlessly via QR codes or custom links generated by the speaker.

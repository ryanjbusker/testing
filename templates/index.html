BMM Translation

Real-time translation for seamless communication

I'm a Speaker I'm in the Audience

BMM Translations is a real-time, browser-based translation platform designed to empower organizations of all sizes to reach multilingual audiences—without the need for expensive hardware, complex installations, or high licensing fees.

Whether you're hosting events, services, workshops, or conferences, BMM Translations makes it easy to connect speakers and listeners across languages through live audio and text translations.

What We Offer

  • Live Translation in Real Time: Our system captures speech from presenters and delivers accurate, sentence-level translations to audience members within moments.
  • Large Audience Support: Capable of supporting hundreds of concurrent users across multiple devices without sacrificing performance.
  • No App Required: Both speakers and audience members join directly through their browsers. No downloads, no setup—just a link or QR code.
  • Quick Listener Access: Audience members can join sessions effortlessly via QR codes or custom links generated by the speaker.

Current Limitations of Our Early Release

BMM Translations is currently in its Limited Viable Product (LVP) phase. While the system is fully functional, we are actively refining and updating the software.

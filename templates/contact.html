About Us

The idea for BMM Translations started in Decemeber of 2024 when our own church had a need for translations and started paying for a professional service at a high price. The next month, we started our Computer Science Senior Capstone project for CSCE 482 at Texas A&M University and decided that this would be the perfect time to create a more affordable translation service for our church. After getting a perfect grade in the class, we continued development through the summer and created an LLC. While we enter this new phase, we are looking for clients willing to partner with us and give helpful feedback to allow us to improve our product. We are dedicated to continuous improvement and are flexible to meet the needs of our clients.
